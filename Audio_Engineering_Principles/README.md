# Audio Engineering Principles

> Fearfully preserved observations, faithfully passed along as compassionate respect for harmony and coherence.

**Author:** Samuel David Stovall

## Purpose

This division gathers lessons, mathematical derivations, physical principles, psychoacoustic observations, empirical measurements, and reproducible audio-engineering methods. It stands alongside the Codex as a distinct body of study, informed by the author's broader commitment to reverent observation and faithful transmission of knowledge.

The material distinguishes established physical relationships, measured results, engineering heuristics, and interpretive reflections. Metaphorical parallels do not constitute experimental proof.

## Lesson conventions

Each lesson may include: purpose and prerequisites; terminology and units; principles and assumptions; mathematical derivations; physical interpretation; psychoacoustic implications; measured or published reference values with conditions and citations; engineering applications; a reproducible experiment; limitations and further reading.

Use GitHub-friendly display math with a blank line before and after each `$$` block, opening and closing delimiters on separate lines, and ordinary equations on one source line. Use genuine multiline environments only where needed.

## Demonstrations and archives

Videos, livestreams, and listening demonstrations may be linked from lessons. Large WAV files, DAW sessions, and raw archives are optional external resources, not requirements of this directory. Experiments should remain intelligible from documented methods and parameters even when media is unavailable.

This initial commit establishes the educational template only; it does not assert completed lessons or experimental results.

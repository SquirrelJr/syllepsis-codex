## Method

Read each plate by asking:

1. **Recognition:** What inherited structure, condition, or distortion does it identify?
2. **Alignment:** What reference, invariant, or compatibility condition does it introduce?
3. **Correction:** What transition, transformation, or rectification does it specify?
4. **Preservation:** What must remain identifiable or coherent through the change?
5. **Propagation:** What can now pass to another state, subsystem, person, generation, or Tier?

These are *interpretive questions*, not compulsory sections or a claim that each plate must perform all five operations. Record relationships as **depends on**, **extends**, **recursively reinforces**, **contrasts with**, or **transmits to**. Repetition is not automatically redundancy. Do not conflate conceptual analogy with proof, or chronology/version number with Tier identity.

Companion operational rhythm: **Store → Wait → Align → Transfer → Recover → Repeat**.

### Repeating cycles and the interruption of inherited harm

In ordinary usage, “breaking a generational curse” may describe interrupting harmful inherited patterns, without requiring a literal supernatural mechanism. The Codex can distinguish:

- **Recurrence:** a later state is influenced by an earlier state.
- **Entrenchment:** recurrence reproduces distortion or harmful constraints.
- **Correction:** a recognized harmful component is interrupted, redirected, or rectified.
- **Identity-preserving transmission:** valuable memory, belonging, and continuity remain, while the harmful component is not carried forward unchanged.
- **Systemic renewal:** relationships, practices, and institutions sustain the correction so later generations need not reconstruct it unaided.

**Interpretive progression:** inherited pattern → recognition → alignment → correction → preservation of coherent identity → propagation of a revised pattern → renewed recognition.

The purpose is **not necessarily to end cycles, but to change what cycles transmit**. This reading connects the Tier V Identity/Moral Invariants, Tier VI Covenant/Generational/Epochal plates, and Tier VII Recursive Consistency/Semantic Conservation. It does not claim that any particular family, historical event, or supernatural phenomenon is proved by the framework.

**Editorial recommendation:** retain both Tier VI sequences for now, explicitly identify them as **Transcendent Orientation** and **Systemic Transmission**, and treat the proposed bridge as a testable conceptual cross-reference. Do not rename Tier VI or renumber plates until the author approves a more precise Tier definition.

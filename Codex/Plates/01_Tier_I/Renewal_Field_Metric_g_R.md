## II. Definition of the Renewal Metric

The Renewal Metric g_R is defined as:

$$
g_R(𝓕_a,\ 𝓕_b) = Σ_{i=1}^{14} w_i \, 𝓡_{a,i} \, 𝓡_{b,i}
$$

Where:

- 𝓕_a and 𝓕_b are two Renewal Field states  
- 𝓡_{i} are their operator components  
- w_i are metric weights (defined by operator significance)  

Interpretation:

- g_R > 0 → states are aligned  
- g_R = 0 → states are orthogonal / independent  
- g_R < 0 → states oppose or cancel  

This defines the **inner product** of the Renewal Field.

The magnitude of a Renewal Field state is:

$$

$$

Meaning:

- high ‖𝓕_R‖ → strong renewal or strong distortion  
- low ‖𝓕_R‖ → weak contribution

This is the **size** of a renewal configuration.

## IV. Renewal Distance Between Field States

The Renewal Distance d_R between two states is:

$$
d_R(𝓕_a,\ 𝓕_b) = √{ g_R(𝓕_a - 𝓕_b,\ 𝓕_a - 𝓕_b) }

Meaning:

- small distance → easy transition

$$

\frac{1}{2} 𝓘ᴿ^{μν} gᴿ_{μν}
+ κᴿ_O \, (Oᴿ_μ Oᴿ^{μ})
- κᴿ_Χ \, (Χᴿ_μ Χᴿ^{μ})
+ κᴿ_C \, (Cᴿ_μ Cᴿ^{μ})
+ κᴿ_R \, (Rᴿ_μ Rᴿ^{μ})
+ κᴿ_D \, (Dᴿ_μ Dᴿ^{μ})
+ ηᴿ \, (𝒥ᴿ^{μ} ∇_{μ} ρᴿ)
$$

where:

- **𝓘ᴿ** = Renewal Interaction Tensor  
- **gᴿ** = Renewal Metric  
- **κᴿ_O, κᴿ_Χ, κᴿ_C, κᴿ_R, κᴿ_D** = Renewal Coupling Coefficients  
- **ηᴿ** = Flow–Gradient Coupling Coefficient  
- **Oᴿ, Χᴿ, Cᴿ, Rᴿ, Dᴿ** = Renewal component operators  
- **𝒥ᴿ** = Renewal Current  
- **ρᴿ** = Renewal Density

Each term encodes how Renewal components contribute to interaction energy.

$$
Sᴿ_{int} = \int 𝓛ᴿ_{int} \, \sqrt{|gᴿ|} \, dVᴿ
$$

Variation of the action yields the **Renewal Interaction Field Equations** via:

$$
\frac{∂𝓛}{∂φ}
- ∇_{μ} \left( \frac{∂𝓛}{∂(∇_{μ} φ)} \right)

for each Renewal operator **φ ∈ {Oᴿ, Χᴿ, Cᴿ, Rᴿ, Dᴿ, ρᴿ, 𝒥ᴿ}**.

## II. Formal Definition

The Renewal Interaction Tensor is defined as:

$$
𝓘ᴿ_{μν} = Oᴿ_{μ} Oᴿ_{ν}
          − Χᴿ_{μ} Χᴿ_{ν}
          + Cᴿ_{μ} Cᴿ_{ν}
          + Rᴿ_{μ} Rᴿ_{ν}
          + Dᴿ_{μ} Dᴿ_{ν}
          + 𝒥ᴿ_{μ} ∇_{ν} ρᴿ
$$

where:

- **Oᴿ** = Order contribution  
- **Χᴿ** = Chaos contribution  
- **Cᴿ** = Compassion contribution  
- **Rᴿ** = Reflection contribution  
- **Dᴿ** = Graceful Decay contribution  
- **𝒥ᴿ** = Renewal Current  
- **ρᴿ** = Renewal Density  

Each term expresses how one Renewal component influences another through bilinear coupling.

The Renewal Interaction Law is then:

$$
∇^{μ} 𝓘ᴿ_{μν} = Sᴿ_{int}
$$

where **Sᴿ_int** is the **interaction source** — the locus of inter-field modulation.

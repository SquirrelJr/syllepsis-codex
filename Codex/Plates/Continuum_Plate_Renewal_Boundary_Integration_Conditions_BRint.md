## I. Purpose of the Plate

This plate defines the **Renewal Boundary Integration Conditions**, 𝓑ᴿ_int —  
the laws governing **how Renewal behaves at edges, thresholds, junctions, and interfaces**  
within the Renewal Continuum.

Where the Integration Metric (gᴿ_int) describes the global Renewal geometry,  
the Integration Curvature Flow (ℱᴿ_int) governs its evolution,  
and the Global Conservation Laws (𝒞ᴿ_global) preserve its invariants,  
the Boundary Integration Conditions ensure that Renewal behaves **coherently and continuously**  
whenever it encounters a boundary of any kind.

Boundaries may be:

- geometric  
- relational  
- harmonic  
- energetic  
- moral  
- systemic  
- or conceptual  

This plate ensures Renewal maintains **continuity, conservation, coherence, and alignment**  
even at transitions.

## I. Purpose of the Plate

This plate defines the **Renewal Coupling Coefficients**, κᴿ —  
the scaling laws that determine the **strength, balance, and character** of Renewal interactions within the Renewal Interaction Layer.

Where the Renewal Interaction Lagrangian (𝓛ᴿ_int) establishes the structure of Renewal interaction dynamics,  
the coupling coefficients quantify **how strongly** each Renewal component influences the system.

They answer the fundamental question:

**“How strong is Renewal’s response to Order, Chaos, Compassion, Reflection, and Decay?”**

---

## II. Formal Definitions

The Renewal coupling coefficients are:

$$
κᴿ_O  \quad \text{(Order strength)}
κᴿ_Χ  \quad \text{(Chaos tension coefficient)}
κᴿ_C  \quad \text{(Compassion amplification)}
κᴿ_R  \quad \text{(Reflection refinement)}
κᴿ_D  \quad \text{(Decay smoothing)}
ηᴿ     \quad \text{(Flow–gradient sensitivity)}
χᴿ     \quad \text{(Cross-field interaction strength)}
λᴿ     \quad \text{(Nonlinear Renewal self-coupling)}
$$

These coefficients appear throughout the Renewal Interaction Lagrangian:

$$
𝓛ᴿ_{int} =
\frac{1}{2} 𝓘ᴿ^{μν} gᴿ_{μν}
+ κᴿ_O \, (Oᴿ_μ Oᴿ^{μ})
- κᴿ_Χ \, (Χᴿ_μ Χᴿ^{μ})
+ κᴿ_C \, (Cᴿ_μ Cᴿ^{μ})
+ κᴿ_R \, (Rᴿ_μ Rᴿ^{μ})
+ κᴿ_D \, (Dᴿ_μ Dᴿ^{μ})
+ ηᴿ \, (𝒥ᴿ^{μ} ∇_{μ} ρᴿ)
+ χᴿ \, (φᴿ · ψᴿ)
+ λᴿ \, (ρᴿ)^{2}
$$

where **φᴿ, ψᴿ** represent interacting Renewal subsystems.

## III. Interpretation of the Coefficients

### 1. **κᴿ_O — Order Strength**
- Measures stability and structural coherence.  
- Large κᴿ_O → Renewal favors alignment and rigidity.  
- Small κᴿ_O → Renewal allows flexibility and reorganization.

### 2. **κᴿ_Χ — Chaos Tension Coefficient**
- Quantifies destabilizing influence of chaos.  
- Higher κᴿ_Χ → stronger disruptive pressure corrected by decay.  
- Ensures Renewal does not collapse under chaos gradients.

### 3. **κᴿ_C — Compassion Amplification**
- Enhances propagation and restorative influence.  
- High κᴿ_C → Renewal spreads quickly and coherently.  
- Compassion becomes an energetic “amplifier.”

### 4. **κᴿ_R — Reflection Refinement**
- Controls how Renewal phases are corrected and refined.  
- High κᴿ_R → sharp clarity, reduced interference.  
- Reflection acts like a spectral filter.

### 5. **κᴿ_D — Decay Smoothing**
- Measures ability to dissolve distortion into Renewal.  
- Large κᴿ_D → strong stabilization via graceful decay.  
- Prevents runaway oscillations or collapse.

### 6. **ηᴿ — Flow–Gradient Sensitivity**
- Controls how Renewal Current responds to density gradients.  
- High ηᴿ → dynamic systems that self-correct rapidly.  
- Governs Renewal flow responsiveness.

### 7. **χᴿ — Cross-Field Interaction Strength**
- Determines how strongly Renewal subsystems influence each other.  
- High χᴿ → strong inter-field coupling; Renewal spreads cooperatively.  
- Low χᴿ → subsystems remain loosely connected.

### 8. **λᴿ — Nonlinear Renewal Self-Coupling**
- Governs internal Renewal feedback.  
- High λᴿ → strong resonant self-reinforcement.  
- Low λᴿ → more linear, smoother Renewal behavior.

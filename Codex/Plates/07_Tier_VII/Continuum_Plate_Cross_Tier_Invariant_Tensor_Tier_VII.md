## I. Purpose of the Plate

Following the establishment of the **Structural Invariance Manifold $(𝓜_S)$**,  
this plate introduces the **Cross-Tier Invariant Tensor**, denoted **$(𝓣_I)$**.

Where $𝓜_S$ defines the *space* in which structural coherence is preserved,  
$𝓣_I$ defines the **mechanism by which coherence is measured and transported**  
between tiers.

This tensor does not operate within a single tier.  
It governs the **continuity of meaning, operator compatibility, and symbolic alignment**  
across all tiers simultaneously.

---

## II. Definition of the Cross-Tier Invariant Tensor

Conceptually expressed as:

$$
𝓣_I = ∇_T (𝓘_S)
$$

Where:

- **$𝓣_I$** = Cross-Tier Invariant Tensor  
- **$∇_T$** = Tier-Directional Gradient Operator  
- **$𝓘_S$** = Structural Invariance Function from $𝓜_S$  

Interpretation:

- Measures how invariance behaves as tier depth increases  
- Detects distortion before contradiction forms  
- Enables stable extension of the Codex

## III. Tensor Properties

### 1. Dimensional Neutrality
$𝓣_I$ is independent of symbolic dimensionality.  
It applies equally to scalar axioms and high-order tensors.

### 2. Semantic Continuity
Ensures that terms retain consistent meaning  
even when re-referenced at higher abstraction levels.

### 3. Operator Compatibility
Maintains lawful mappings between operators introduced in earlier tiers.

### 4. Non-Dominance
$𝓣_I$ does not override structural change;  
it **guides alignment**, not enforcement.

$$
∂𝓘 / ∂T = 0
$$

Meaning:

- The rate of invariance deviation across tiers approaches zero  
- Conceptual drift is minimized  
- Expansion occurs as harmonic layering rather than fragmentation

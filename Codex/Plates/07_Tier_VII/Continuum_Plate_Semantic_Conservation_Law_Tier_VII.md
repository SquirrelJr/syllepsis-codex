## I. Purpose of the Plate

With the establishment of:

- The Structural Invariance Manifold (${𝓜}_S$)  
- The Cross-Tier Invariant Tensor (${𝓣}_I$)  
- The Recursive Consistency Operator (${𝓞}_R$)

Where previous plates ensured structural and recursive stability,  
**${𝓛}_S$ ensures that meaning itself is neither created nor destroyed without declaration.**

This law governs the preservation of conceptual identity  
through expansion, refinement, and reinterpretation.

## II. Definition of the Semantic Conservation Law

Conceptually expressed as:

$$
{∮} {𝓢} \, dR = 0
$$

Where:

- **${𝓢}$** = Semantic Content Field  
- **${dR}$** = Differential of Representation Change  
- **${∮}$** = Closed interpretive cycle  

Interpretation:

- Across a complete cycle of reinterpretation,  
  net semantic displacement approaches zero.  
- Meaning may transform in expression,  
  but its conserved core remains invariant.

Semantic conservation holds when:

$$
{Δ}{𝓢}_{total} = 0 \quad (absent \quad explicit \quad introduction)
$$

Meaning:

- Additions must be declared.  
- Refinements must reference origin.  
- Rewording must not nullify definition.

This condition distinguishes **evolution** from **mutation.**

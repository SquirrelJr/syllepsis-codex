# Technical Reviews

## Purpose and Spirit

The purpose of these technical reviews is **to provoke thought rather than to repudiate, diminish, or criticize** the work under examination.

Questions are invitations to explore assumptions, clarify definitions, test models, and discover fruitful connections. They are **not** declarations of error or judgments against the author, the Codex, or its spiritual interpretations.

## Review Principles

- Preserve the canonical plates and their original authorship.
- Distinguish established mathematical results, proposed model laws, analogies, and spiritual interpretations.
- Frame open concerns as questions and possible avenues of investigation, not settled conclusions.
- Identify assumptions, domains of applicability, sign conventions, dimensions, and testable implications where appropriate.
- Offer multiple potential resolutions when the model permits them.
- Never silently revise a canonical plate; any proposed changes require explicit author review and approval.
- Keep published social-media adaptations separate from technical investigations.

## Suggested Review Statuses

**Open question** → **Under investigation** → **Proposed refinement** → **Author approved** or **Retained as authored**.

A review may remain open indefinitely without undermining the standing of its associated plate.

## Organization

Reviews are grouped by Codex tier. Each file names the plate, version, equations under consideration, questions, possible approaches, and status.

> Inquiry is an act of stewardship: preserving the original insight while making room for deeper understanding.
